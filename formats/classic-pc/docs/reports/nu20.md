## 2. Causes found

1. **Model lists read one entry off** (measured). Each model lost its first entry and gained one from its
   neighbour. Effects: in one-mesh models the mesh was lost altogether (this is why the three part files
   gave no glTF); meshes paired with the wrong material, so the format size disagreed and the reader fell
   back to positions only. Batman `chars\batman\batman_pc.ghg`: 37 of 104 meshes had a fitting material
   before, 104 of 104 now.
2. **Two wrong field sizes in the vertex format** (measured): packed blend indices are 4 bytes, and 0x80 is
   a 4-byte slot.
3. **An older mesh record layout** in 4 leftover files (measured): all 57 mesh errors.
4. **Vertices without a position** (flag 0x10000000) in 8 swarm-animal meshes (measured): the 3 Batman and
   5 of the Indy "absurd position" meshes.
5. **The remaining "absurd" positions are what the files hold** (measured, `baduv.py`, `residue.py`): TCS 271
   and Indy 23 meshes. In all of them the other attributes are sound (normals unit, or UVs sane where there
   are no normals), so the layout is right. 187 of the TCS ones are boxes or planes with coordinates near
   50,000 (e.g. MAUL_B mesh 279: a cube from -50,000 to +50,000); 76 are domes or rings at 10,000 to 99,999.
   That they are sky and far-background shapes is a guess from their shape. A handful sit at 1e6 to 1e14
   (e.g. DEATHSTARESCAPE_B mesh 28: a 9 cm part with every y = 2e14): guessed to be things parked out of sight.
6. **Remaining bad UVs are what the files hold** (measured): 79 of 80 belong to materials with no texture
   (the UV slot is not used); the one textured mesh (Indy caferaven_outro mesh 392) has 72 of 627 vertices
   with the same nonsense pair in both UV sets.
7. **Remaining non-unit normals are what the files hold** (measured): the bytes are exactly 7f/80 (a zero
   vector) in 124 of 157 meshes, short vectors in the rest (e.g. DROIDTRIFIGHTER: 80 80 5d).
8. **Textures "not written"** (measured on all 55,708 texture entries): every entry without data is one of
   the five that follow a cubemap (TCS 1,195 = 5 x 239; Indy 770 = 5 x 154; Batman 265 = 5 x 53) or one of
   the 57 in the payload-less Indy file. No pixels are missing; the faces are inside the cubemap's DDS.
   Separately, Pillow cannot open the float format, so those 49 had no PNG.

## 5. The two files that did not parse

- **Indy `...\outtakes_losttemple_outrowin_pc.gsc`** (measured): a TCS-style file (first 4 bytes = offset of
  the NU20 block) whose offset says 36,452,288 while the file is 204,996 bytes: 4 + the 204,864-byte NU20
  block + the usual 128-byte trailer ("NUS: 15:35:13 06-08-2007 Converted: 15:36:07 06-08-2007"). The
  textures and vertex / index buffers are simply not in it. Now read as a scene description (57 texture
  entries, 122 materials, 245 meshes, 142 models, 17 named objects such as Cut_axe, Idol); every mesh
  reports "vertex buffer N does not exist". Whether the game's own archive holds it this short, or the
  extraction cut it, I did not check.
- **Batman `chars\joker\joker.hgo_pc.gsc`** (measured): container version 1, and the NU20 block IS the whole
  file (2,246,912 bytes). Four DXT3 textures (two 1024 x 1024) lie inside the TST0 chunk and the vertex /
  index data inside VBIB; the scene header fields are arranged differently; bone names (upperTorso,
  leftKnee, weaponLeft, toeTwist) are not LEGO Batman's skeleton. Guess: a leftover in an older TT format.
  Still not read; the reader now says so in one sentence instead of a struct error.
